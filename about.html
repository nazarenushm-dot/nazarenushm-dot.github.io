About

Hi, I'm Hannah.

I'm a Nebraska journalist who tells stories about the people, places and policies that shape life in the Midwest.

Who I am

I'm finishing my Bachelor of Journalism at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln this December, with a minor in Political Science. I report on Nebraska because I care how people here live, work and vote.

Right now I'm a news intern at Nebraska Public Media, where I produce stories for digital and broadcast. I pitch my own ideas, interview sources and turn my reporting into broadcast-ready scripts. Before that, I wrote opinion for The Daily Nebraskan and covered breaking news and local government at the North Platte Telegraph.
